Nature of This Project

BDS-07 is an academic capstone project. It is not a commercial service, product, or application. The website, API, and dashboard are built for educational and demonstration purposes as part of the T.Y. B.Sc. Data Science curriculum, Semester V.

Predictions Are Not Medical Advice

The PM2.5 predictions generated by this system are outputs of a machine learning model trained on limited historical data for three Indian cities. They should not be used as the sole basis for health decisions, medical planning, or regulatory compliance. Always consult official air quality monitors and medical professionals for health-related decisions.

No Warranty

This project is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Predictions may be inaccurate due to model limitations, sensor dropout, API downtime, or changes in atmospheric conditions outside the training distribution.
